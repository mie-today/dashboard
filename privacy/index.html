Privacy Policy

Kairoks · Effective date: August 13, 2026 · Last updated: August 13, 2026

Our core promise: Your data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. Kairoks has no ads. We never read your email inbox. Your information exists for one purpose only — to organize it for you.

1. Who we are

Kairoks ("we," "us," or "our") is an information organization service operated from San Francisco, CA, United States. Kairoks lets you send information to your Kairoks address by email, or enter it through the Kairoks web interface, and then organizes it into your calendar, documents, and personal dashboards (Finance, Health, Tasks, Projects).

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@kairoks.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name, your Google account email address, and your basic Google profile information (such as your first name, used to address you in the interface).

Content you send us. The notes, records, and messages you send to your Kairoks address or enter through the Kairoks interface. This may include schedule information, financial records, health-related records, tasks, projects, and memos that you choose to send.

Google account data. With your explicit consent via Google OAuth, we access your Google Calendar, Google Tasks, and your Google account identity. We request only the minimum scopes required for these functions, listed in full in section 3.

Usage data. Basic technical information such as sign-in timestamps, device type, and feature usage, used solely to operate and improve the service.

3. Google user data: scopes and how we use them

Kairoks requests the following Google OAuth scopes. We request no others, and we use each one only for the purpose described here.

ScopeWhat Kairoks does with it
calendar.readonly Lists the calendars in your account so you can choose which ones Kairoks displays, reads events from the calendars you select, and reads Google's event colour definitions so events render correctly.
calendar.events Creates, updates, and deletes calendar events on your behalf — for example, turning a note you send into a scheduled event. Kairoks writes events only to your primary calendar.
gmail.send Sends a single message, composed by you in the Kairoks interface, from your Google account to your Kairoks address. This scope grants send access only; it does not grant permission to read, list, or modify any message in your mailbox.
tasks Reads your Google Tasks lists and items, and creates or updates tasks when you add or complete them in Kairoks.
userinfo.email Identifies your account so your data is associated with the correct user.
userinfo.profile Retrieves your first name so the interface can address you by name.
openid Standard sign-in with Google.

4. What we do not access

Some limits are worth stating explicitly, because they are the questions people ask most often:

  • We do not read your email. Kairoks makes no request to read, list, search, or download any message in your mailbox. We hold no scope that would permit it.
  • We do not monitor your mailbox. Kairoks registers no change notifications, watches, or push subscriptions on any user's Gmail account. The only mailbox Kairoks monitors is our own service address, which receives the messages you deliberately send to it.
  • We do not read your email metadata — no headers, no subject lines, no sender or recipient lists, no labels.
  • We do not access your Google Drive or any file stored in it. The spreadsheets Kairoks uses to organize your records are created and owned by Kairoks, not stored in your Drive.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location history, or any Google service not listed in section 3.

5. How we use your information

  • To parse, classify, and organize the content you send into your calendar, documents, and dashboards
  • To create and sync calendar events with your Google Calendar
  • To create and update items in your Google Tasks
  • To deliver schedule summaries to you
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the service
  • To communicate with you about your account and service updates

6. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data — to anyone, for any reason
  • We never share your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • We never use your data — including any health-related records — for advertising or marketing profiles
  • We never display ads in any Kairoks product
  • We never use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • We never transfer Google user data to third parties, except as required by law or as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features of Kairoks

7. Health-related information

Some users choose to send health-related records (such as weight, blood pressure, or glucose readings) to Kairoks. We treat all such records as sensitive information. They are encrypted at rest, are never shared with any third party, are never used for advertising, and are accessible only to you. Kairoks is a personal record-keeping tool and is not a medical device; it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

8. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud Platform infrastructure in the United States. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using TLS. OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted with Google Cloud Key Management Service before storage. Internal access to user data is restricted to the minimum necessary for operating the service, controlled through identity and access management policies, and logged.

Please note that content you send to your Kairoks address travels as ordinary internet email, which is not an end-to-end encrypted channel. Your message is encrypted once it reaches our systems, but its delivery depends on the security of the mail providers it passes through. If you prefer, you may enter information through the Kairoks web interface instead, which is served over TLS end to end.

9. Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual records at any time within the service. If you delete your account, all associated personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

You can revoke Kairoks' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further access to your Google data immediately.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct, and the right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any right, contact contact@kairoks.com. We respond within the timelines required by applicable law.

11. Google API Services disclosure

Kairoks' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the Kairoks interface, is never transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition, is never used for advertising, is never used to develop or train generalized AI or ML models, and is never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. No humans read Google user data unless we have your affirmative consent, it is necessary for security purposes or to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

12. Children's privacy

Kairoks is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by email or in-service notice at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
