1. Who we are

Your privacy is important to us at Kairoks. We respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you across our Service.

Kairoks ("Kairoks," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Kairoks service (the "Service") at kairoks.com. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Kairoks sorts the messages you send it into a calendar and into dashboard categories. This policy explains what information the Service collects, how it is used, who it is disclosed to, and what choices you have.

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@kairoks.com.

2. Information we gather

Our primary goal in collecting information is to provide the Service and improve it, to administer your use of the Service and your account, and to enable you to enjoy and easily navigate the Service.

Account information. We collect the information that you voluntarily provide when you sign up for the Service. The information we collect is your name, email address, and phone number. When you sign in with Google, we also collect the name, email address, and basic profile information that Google returns to us as part of that sign-in.

Contact details for messaging. When you use a message channel, we collect the messaging handle or phone number you register, which we use to recognize the messages you send. We collect the messages themselves, including text and any attachments, together with the timestamp at which each message was received.

Support correspondence. We collect the messages you send us for support and the contact details you use to send them.

Information collected automatically. Our servers automatically record certain information about how the Service is used ("Log Data"). Log Data consists of sign-in timestamps, IP address, browser and device type, operating system, the pages and features viewed and the time spent on them, and error reports. We use this information to operate, administer, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, and to generate aggregate, non-identifying statistics about how the Service is used.

Approximate location. The only location information we hold is what can be inferred from your IP address, which gives a rough, city-level approximation. We use it for security purposes, such as recognizing unusual sign-in activity. We do not request or collect precise location, and we do not request access to your device's microphone, contacts, camera, photos, or stored files. We do not collect biometric information.

Information we do not collect. We do not buy personal information about you from data brokers, and we do not collect information about you from advertising or marketing partners.

3. How we use your information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To sort the messages you send into categories such as Calendar, Finance, Health, Tasks, and Projects, and to display them on your dashboard
  • To authenticate your sign-in and confirm that your account has access to the Service
  • To create, update, and delete events in your calendar where you have connected one
  • To send you messages, such as summaries you have asked for, account notices, and security alerts
  • To process your subscription payments and provide receipts
  • To operate, maintain, secure, debug, and improve the Service, including monitoring for fraud and abuse
  • To respond to your support requests and to your privacy rights requests
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

We do not use the content of your messages to build advertising or marketing profiles, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. Message channels

You can send messages to Kairoks through the message channels the Service supports and through the Kairoks web interface. If you register a phone number and opt in to receive text messages from Kairoks at that number, the following applies.

Consent. You opt in by registering your mobile number in your Kairoks account and confirming the verification message we send to it. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchasing anything.

What we send. We send account and service messages only — verification codes, the summaries you have enabled, and notices about your account. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

Message frequency. Message frequency varies and depends on how often you use the Service.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier, not Kairoks, sets those charges.

Stopping messages. You can stop messages at any time by removing your number in your account settings. Opting out of messages does not delete your account or your existing messages.

Carrier limitations. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No sharing of message consent. Your mobile number and your consent to receive messages are never sold, and are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

Security note. Messages sent to Kairoks over mobile carrier networks are not end-to-end encrypted. Messages are encrypted once they reach our systems, but transmission across carrier networks is subject to the security of those networks. If you would rather not send sensitive information that way, use the Kairoks web interface instead.

Consent. Where you have given it — for example, connecting your Google account, or opting in to receive text messages. You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

Legal obligation. Where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

7. Google API Services and Limited Use

Signing in with Google, and connecting your calendar, are optional. If you use them, the following applies.

What we request

We request the following scopes:

openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile — to authenticate you and identify your account by email address and display name.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to read, create, update, and delete the calendar events that correspond to the messages you send, so that your dashboard and your calendar stay consistent with each other.

We request the narrowest scopes that allow these features to work. We do not request access to your Gmail mailbox, your Google Drive files, or your Google contacts, and we cannot read them. We do not access calendars other than the ones you connect, and we do not modify calendar settings, sharing permissions, or calendars themselves.

What we write to your calendar

When a message you send is classified as scheduling information, or as a record you have asked to track over time, Kairoks creates a corresponding event in the Google Calendar you connected. The content of that event is drawn from your message, so it may include personal details — for example, a recorded measurement or a reminder relating to your own records.

These events are written to your own calendar under your own Google account. Kairoks does not create events in anyone else's calendar and does not change the sharing settings of any calendar. Once an event exists in your calendar, it is held by Google under your Google account and is governed by Google's own privacy policy, and it is visible to anyone you have granted access to that calendar. If you do not want a particular category of information written to your calendar, turn that category off in your Kairoks settings before sending it, or send it through a channel you have not connected to a calendar.

Limited Use

Kairoks' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data obtained through these scopes is:

  • used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the Kairoks interface;
  • not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition as described in section 14, and in each case subject to the Limited Use requirements;
  • never used for serving advertising, including personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising;
  • never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • never sold; and
  • not read by any human, except with your explicit consent for a specific issue you have raised, where it is necessary for security purposes or to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and de-identified.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Kairoks from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by disconnecting it in your Kairoks account settings. Revoking access stops future calendar syncing; events already created in your calendar remain in your calendar until you delete them there.

8. Cookies and tracking technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a web server when you access the Service. Kairoks uses cookies and equivalent browser storage that are strictly necessary to run the Service: keeping you signed in, remembering your session, and protecting against cross-site request forgery. Session cookies are deleted when you sign out and close your browser; persistent cookies remain so that you do not have to sign in on every visit.

Most browsers accept cookies automatically, and you can change your browser settings to refuse them or to prompt you first. Because the cookies we use are strictly necessary, blocking them will prevent you from signing in.

We do not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, web beacons, or third-party ad-network trackers, and we do not permit third parties to place tracking technologies on the Service for advertising. Because we do not use tracking technologies for advertising or for the sale or sharing of personal information, there is nothing to opt out of under the state laws that give you that right.

Do Not Track. There is no finalized industry standard for responding to browser Do Not Track signals, and we do not respond to them. We do honor Global Privacy Control signals where applicable law requires it.

9. Payment information

Paid subscriptions are processed by third-party payment processors: Stripe, PayPal, and Google Pay. When you pay, you provide your payment details directly to the processor you have chosen. Kairoks does not receive or store your full card number, bank account number, or card security code.

We receive from the processor only what we need to manage your subscription: a payment identifier, the outcome of the transaction, the amount and currency, the date, and the last four digits and brand of the payment method. Each processor handles your payment information under its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe — stripe.com/privacy
  • PayPal — paypal.com/us/legalhub/privacy-full
  • Google Pay — payments.google.com privacy notice

11. How long we keep your information

Your messages are retained for as long as your account is active, because accumulated history is the point of the Service. You may delete individual records at any time within the Service, and you can access and modify the personal information associated with your account through your account settings.

If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information and message content from our active systems within 30 days. Copies may persist in encrypted backups until they are overwritten in the normal backup rotation; during that period the data is isolated from any further processing.

We retain a limited set of records beyond deletion only where the law requires it — for example, transaction and tax records associated with payments, and records needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. These records do not include the content of your messages.

Events that Kairoks created in your own calendar are stored by your calendar provider under your own account, not by us. Deleting your Kairoks account, or disconnecting your Google account, does not delete them, because after disconnection we no longer have the access needed to remove them. You can delete them yourself in your calendar at any time. If you would like them removed before you disconnect, ask us at contact@kairoks.com while your account is still connected.

Please be aware, however, that no method of transmitting information over the internet and no method of storing information is completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of any information, and you send information to the Service at your own risk. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where applicable law requires it, within the timeframes that law sets.
