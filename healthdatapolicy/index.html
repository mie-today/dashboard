Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Kairoks · Effective date: August 26, 2026 · Last updated: August 26, 2026

In short: Kairoks never sells your health records and never uses them for advertising or marketing. We collect only what is needed to give you the features you asked for. Sharing your health records with anyone outside Kairoks happens only if you turn it on, and you can turn it off again at any time. If you ask us to delete your health records, we delete them permanently and they cannot be recovered.

1. Purpose and scope of this notice

This is Kairoks' Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice. It is a separate document from our Privacy Policy and from our Terms of Use, and it exists because the Washington My Health My Data Act and the Nevada Consumer Health Data Privacy Act each require a standalone notice about health data. Nothing in our Terms of Use limits the rights this notice gives you. It describes what health data Kairoks collects, why we collect it, where it comes from, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

We apply this notice to everyone who uses Kairoks, wherever you live. We do not ask you to prove which state or country you are in before you can exercise the rights described here, and we do not give some users fewer rights than others.

Health data means the records you keep in the Health part of Kairoks, and any record you send us that describes your physical or mental health. Section 3 lists the categories in full. Where this notice and our Privacy Policy both speak to the same health record, this notice governs.

Two things are outside this notice. The first is information that has nothing to do with health — your calendar entries about meetings, your Finance records, your tasks and projects. The second is the billing records created when you pay for a subscription. A payment record shows that you paid us, not anything about your health, so it is handled under the Privacy Policy rather than here. That distinction matters in one practical place: when we delete your health data we delete all of it, but we may still keep the billing records that tax law requires us to keep.

2. Kairoks and how to reach us

Kairoks ("Kairoks," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Kairoks service at kairoks.com. We decide the purposes and the means by which the health data described here is collected and processed, which makes us the party responsible for it under the Washington and Nevada laws named above.

Kairoks is an online service and email is how we receive every kind of request under this notice. Write to us at contact@kairoks.com. There is no separate address, form, or phone line to go through — questions, rights requests, and complaints all go to that one address.

Kairoks is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device, it does not give medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and nothing in it should be used in place of a conversation with a clinician.

3. The health data we collect

We collect the following categories of health data. Everything in this list comes from you: you either type it into Kairoks or send it to us in a message. We do not collect any of it in the background, and we do not obtain any of it from anyone other than you.

Body measurements and vital signs. Weight, height, heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen saturation, and body temperature.

Blood sugar and other test results. Glucose readings and cholesterol values, together with the time of each reading. Kairoks also lets you record test results of your own choosing under a name you enter yourself, such as a lab value your doctor tracks for you. Whatever you enter in those fields is health data and is treated the same way as the rest of this list.

Medication records. The names and doses of the medications you take, including insulin and oral medications, the times you take them, when you started them, and any notes you add. If you choose to record the name of the doctor who prescribed a medication, we collect that too. That field is optional and is empty unless you fill it in.

Reproductive and pregnancy-related records. Whether you have recorded a pregnancy, the day of your cycle, and your expected period and ovulation dates, along with any notes you add to a day.

Mood and mental state. The mood you record for a day, the energy level you record with it, and any notes you attach.

Sleep. Bedtime, wake time, the resulting duration, how you rated the night, and any notes.

Physical activity. The type of activity, its duration, sets, repetitions, weight lifted, step count, distance, and the calories the service estimates from what you entered.

Food and water. The foods you log with their meal time, the calorie and nutrient figures attached to them, your water intake, and any notes.

Your health profile and goals. Date of birth, sex, blood type, allergies, activity level, the weight you started at and the weight you are aiming for, the calorie, water and sleep targets you set, and the estimates the service calculates from those figures. This section also includes an emergency contact field. It is optional, and if you fill it in you are giving us information about another person, so please only enter someone who is willing for you to do that.

Precise location. The Health page shows the current weather where you are. To do that, your browser asks you for permission and, if you allow it, gives the page your coordinates. Those coordinates go to the weather service named in section 8 and nowhere else. We do not store them, we do not attach them to any of your health records, and we do not use them to work out anything about your health. If you refuse the permission, or your browser does not offer it, nothing is sent and the weather simply does not appear.

Whatever else you choose to send. Several of the fields above are free text, and the messages you send us are free text as well. If you write something about your health in one of them, it becomes part of your health data under this notice, whether or not it fits neatly into a category above.

4. Why we collect each category

The purpose of every category listed in section 3 is the same and it is a narrow one: to record what you sent, to sort it into the right place in Kairoks, to show it back to you on your dashboard, and to keep it there so you can see how it changes over time. Building that record is the service you signed up for, and it is the only reason the data exists in our systems.

Three purposes sit alongside that, each attached to a specific feature.

When you log a food by name, Kairoks looks that name up in a public nutrition database so it can fill in calories and nutrients for you instead of making you type them. When you ask Kairoks to put a record in your calendar, we create the corresponding event there. When your browser gives us your coordinates, we use them to show the current weather on the page and for nothing else.

We do not analyze your health data to draw conclusions about you, we do not score or profile you, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Where the data comes from

There are three sources and no others.

The first is you, typing directly into the Health pages of Kairoks. The second is you, sending a message to Kairoks; those messages are read and sorted into the categories in section 3. The third is your browser, which supplies your coordinates for the weather display if you allow it to.

We do not buy health data about you from data brokers. We do not receive it from advertising or marketing partners. We do not take it from your other applications, from public records, or from any other outside source. Nobody other than you can add a health record to your Kairoks account.

6. Consent to collection

This section is about collection. Section 7 is about sharing, and the two are deliberately kept apart: agreeing to one has no effect on the other, and neither one is a condition of using Kairoks or of paying for it.

We collect health data only where it is necessary to provide the feature you have asked us for. When you type a glucose reading into the Health page, or send us a message recording one, you are asking us to keep that reading for you, and keeping it is what we do with it. We do not collect health data you have not given us, and we do not use what you gave us for one feature to power something you did not ask for.

If we ever want to collect a category of health data that is not listed in section 3, or to use health data for a purpose that is not described in section 4, we will tell you what the new category or purpose is and ask for your consent first. Until you give it, the new collection or use does not happen. Consent of that kind will always be a separate, clearly worded request that you have to actively agree to, never a pre-ticked box and never something bundled into a longer agreement.

This matters for one feature in particular. Kairoks does not currently use artificial intelligence or large language models to process your health data. We are considering adding it. If we do, we will describe exactly what would be processed and by whom, and we will ask for your consent before any of your health data is used that way.

7. Consent to sharing

Sharing your health data outside Kairoks requires your separate, prior, opt-in consent. It is asked for on its own, it is not combined with your agreement to this notice or with any other permission, and it is off until you turn it on. Declining costs you nothing else: the rest of Kairoks works exactly as before.

There are two things you can consent to, and you can consent to one without the other.

Sending records to your Google Calendar. Turning this on lets Kairoks create calendar events from the records you send it. The event is written to your own Google Calendar under your own Google account, and its content comes from your record, so it can contain the health details you wrote. Section 8 explains what that means in practice.

Looking up foods in nutrition databases. Turning this on lets Kairoks send the name of a food you are logging to the public nutrition databases named in section 8, so that calories and nutrients can be filled in automatically. Only the food name is sent.

Before you agree to either one, you are told which data would be shared, who would receive it, what it would be used for, and how to withdraw your consent. Section 12 explains how to withdraw.

The weather display described in section 3 works differently and is not part of these two choices. Your coordinates are handled entirely by your browser's own location permission, which you grant or refuse in the browser and can change there at any time. We never receive them unless you have said yes to that prompt.

8. Who we share health data with

We share health data with the recipients below and with nobody else. Kairoks has no affiliates, no parent company, and no subsidiaries, so there are no affiliates on this list. If you would like the list of the specific recipients that have actually received data of yours, ask us and we will produce it for your account; section 10 explains how.

Kairoks is an independent service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, and it is not a Google product or partner. The same holds for every other service named below: listing them here describes what they receive from us and nothing more. None of them operates Kairoks, and none of them has any relationship with us beyond the single request described in its entry.

Google. Receives calendar events created from your records, if you have consented under section 7. What is shared is the content of those records. The events are written to your own calendar under your own Google account, so once they exist Google holds them under your account and under Google's own privacy policy, not ours. Kairoks creates these events with their visibility set to private. That hides the details from people who can only see whether you are free or busy, but anyone you have given full access to that calendar — a partner, an assistant, a colleague — can still read them. If you share your calendar with someone, assume they can see anything Kairoks writes into it. Google can be reached at support.google.com/policies.

USDA FoodData Central. A nutrition database run by the United States Department of Agriculture, in the United States. Receives the name of a food you are logging, if you have consented under section 7, so that its nutrition figures can be returned to you. The request is made by our servers rather than by your browser, and it carries nothing that identifies you — no name, no email address, no account identifier, and not your device's network address. This is a lookup in a public reference database, not a report to a government agency about you. It can be reached at fdc.nal.usda.gov/contact.

Open Food Facts. A public food database run by a non-profit organization in France. Receives the name of a food you are logging, on the same terms and with the same limits as the entry above. Because it is operated from France, that lookup leaves the United States. It can be reached at world.openfoodfacts.org/contact.

Open-Meteo. A weather service operated from Germany. Receives the coordinates your browser gives the Health page, and only if you allowed that browser permission. It receives no health record of any kind and nothing that identifies you as a Kairoks user. Because it is operated from Germany, that request leaves the United States. It can be reached at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

Separately from the recipients above, we may disclose health data if the law compels us to — a subpoena, a court order, a warrant, or a comparable lawful demand — or where disclosure is necessary to protect someone from a risk of death or serious physical injury. We review any such demand for validity and scope, and we tell you before we disclose anything unless we are legally forbidden from telling you or a genuine emergency makes it impossible. Disclosures of this kind are not something you can consent to or refuse in advance, and we do not treat them as sharing under section 7.

9. What we never do

We never sell your health data. Not for money, and not in exchange for anything else of value. Both Washington and Nevada allow a sale to go ahead if the company first obtains a signed authorization from you, and Kairoks has no such authorization process because there is nothing for it to authorize. If that ever changed, it could only happen with a separate signed document from you, and we would have to build that process before asking.

We never share your health data for advertising or marketing, by us or by anyone else. Kairoks shows no advertisements. We do not build advertising or marketing profiles from your records, we do not use them for targeted advertising, and we do not use tracking technologies for advertising purposes.

We never buy information about you from data brokers, and we never receive it from advertising partners.

We never operate a geofence around a hospital, clinic, pharmacy, or any other place that provides in-person health care, and we never use one to identify or track anyone who goes near such a place, to send them advertising, or to collect health data from them. Both Washington and Nevada prohibit this, and it is not something Kairoks does or has any use for.

We never use artificial intelligence or large language models to process your health data. That is the position today, and section 6 describes what would have to happen before it could change.

We never treat you differently for exercising any right in this notice. Your subscription price does not change, your features do not change, and the quality of the service you receive does not change.

10. Your rights

These rights belong to you regardless of where you live.

To confirm and to access. You can ask whether we are collecting, sharing, or selling health data about you, and you can ask for a copy of what we hold. We provide it in a form you can read and keep.

To a list of recipients. You can ask for a list of every third party and affiliate that has received health data of yours, and we produce that list for your account specifically rather than pointing you at a general list. It includes a way to contact each recipient, and section 8 gives those contact points as they stand today.

To delete. You can ask us to delete your health data. You can ask for all of it or for part of it — a single record, a date range, or an entire category such as your reproductive and pregnancy-related records. Section 11 describes exactly what happens when you do.

To withdraw consent. You can withdraw your consent to collection and, separately, your consent to sharing. Section 12 describes what each withdrawal does.

To be free of retaliation. Exercising any of these rights costs you nothing, as described in section 9.

How to exercise them

Email contact@kairoks.com from the address on your Kairoks account and say what you want — for example "Confirm," "Access," "List of recipients," "Delete everything," "Delete my pregnancy records," or "Withdraw sharing consent." Plain language is fine; you do not need to quote a statute or use a particular form of words.

We verify that the request really comes from you before acting on it, using the information already attached to your account. If we cannot match you that way we may ask you for something more, and anything you send for that purpose is used for verification and then discarded. Someone else may make a request for you if you have authorized them to; we may ask them to show that authorization and may ask you to confirm it directly.

We answer within 45 days. If a request turns out to be complicated we may take up to 45 days more, and we will tell you inside the first 45 days if that happens. Deletion is faster and is not extendable: section 11 explains that it is completed within 30 days.

11. Deletion and how long it takes

When you ask us to delete everything, we delete your health records and the health-related files that hold them. The deletion is permanent. We do not keep an archived copy for our own use, we do not keep a copy in a separate system, and the earlier versions of your records go with them. There is one narrow exception, and it is a technical one: we keep short-lived operational backups so that we can recover from a system failure. Those backups are overwritten on a rolling seven-day cycle, so a deleted health record disappears from them within seven days of the deletion, well inside the 30 days set out below. We never restore a deleted health record from a backup, and we never search backups for any other purpose. Once that cycle has passed nothing is retained, and we cannot restore your history later even if you ask us to and even if you come back to Kairoks. Kairoks does not provide an export feature, so there is nothing to download before a deletion. Your right to ask us for a copy of your health data is in section 10 and is unaffected by anything here; access and deletion are separate requests and we act on each one as you make it.

When you ask us to delete only part of your health data, we remove the records you named and then rebuild the underlying file so that the earlier versions of those records are gone as well. Your remaining records are unaffected and your account continues to work normally. This takes a little more work at our end than a full deletion, but the result is the same: the deleted records are not recoverable from any earlier version.

Either kind of deletion is completed within 30 days of the day we verify your request.

Your health data is also deleted when your account itself comes to an end. Kairoks does not offer a free or read-only account, so when a paid subscription ends your access ends with it and your health data is deleted 30 days after that date. We email you when your access ends and tell you the deletion date. Subscribing again does not restore what was there; a new subscription starts a new and empty account. If a request under section 10 reaches us within 29 days of that email, we put the deletion on hold, answer the request in full, and then carry the deletion out; the deletion date moves rather than the request being cut short. A request that arrives after your health data has been deleted cannot be answered, because at that point we no longer hold it. That deletion works exactly as described above: it is permanent, and we cannot restore your records afterwards. Our Terms of Use set out the timetable in full.

The original message stays with a copy of what you sent. When you send us a record in a message, the message itself may remain in Kairoks' systems after the record has been sorted into your dashboard. A deletion request covers that copy too, and it is deleted along with everything else.

We also tell the recipients. Where we have shared health data of yours with anyone listed in section 8, we notify them of your deletion request and direct them to delete what they received. There is one limit on this that you should know about. Calendar events sit in your own Google account, not in ours. While your Google account is still connected to Kairoks we can remove events we created; if you have already disconnected it, we no longer have the access needed to reach them and you will need to delete them in your calendar yourself. If you want them removed by us, ask before you disconnect.

The one thing a deletion does not reach is your billing records, for the reason given in section 1. Those records show payments, not health information, and tax law requires us to keep them.

12. Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw either consent at any time by emailing contact@kairoks.com, and you can withdraw one without touching the other.

Withdrawing your sharing consent stops the sharing from that point on. No further calendar events are created from your records, and no further food lookups are sent out. Records you have already saved stay in Kairoks and your account continues to work. Calendar events that were already created remain in your own calendar until you delete them there or ask us to, as described in section 11.

Withdrawing your consent to collection stops us collecting new health data from you. Because collecting your records is what the Health part of Kairoks does, withdrawing this consent means those features stop working — there is nothing left for them to do. Your existing records are not deleted by a withdrawal; if you want them gone, ask for deletion under section 11, which is a separate request.

Withdrawing consent does not make anything we did beforehand unlawful, and it has no effect on any part of Kairoks outside health data.

For the browser location permission behind the weather display, withdrawal is in your hands rather than ours: change the location setting for kairoks.com in your browser and the coordinates stop being sent. You do not need to tell us.

13. Appeals and complaints

If we turn down a request you made under section 10, we tell you why, and you can appeal. Reply to our decision with the word "Appeal" and we will look at it again. We respond within 45 days with the outcome and our reasons, and if we still decline we tell you how to take it further.

You can complain to a regulator whether or not you have appealed to us first.

The Washington State Attorney General accepts consumer complaints at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint. The Washington My Health My Data Act is enforced under that state's Consumer Protection Act, which also allows individuals to bring their own action.

The Nevada Attorney General accepts consumer complaints at ag.nv.gov/Complaints/File_Complaint. Violations of the Nevada Consumer Health Data Privacy Act are treated as deceptive trade practices and are enforced by that office.

If you live elsewhere, your own state attorney general or data protection authority will accept complaints about how we have handled your data, and we will cooperate with them.

14. Users under 18

Kairoks is for adults. We do not knowingly collect health data from anyone under 18, and by using the service you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. If we find out that someone under 18 has an account, we deactivate it and delete the health data associated with it as soon as we can. If you believe someone under 18 has given us health data, tell us at contact@kairoks.com.

15. Changes to this notice

We may revise this notice. The "Last updated" date above shows the most recent revision, and the previous version is available if you ask for it.

Adding a new category of health data, or a new purpose, or a new recipient is not something we can do by quietly editing this page. As sections 6 and 7 explain, we have to tell you what is changing and obtain your consent before the change applies to your data. Where a change does not need your consent, we notify you by email and in the service at least 30 days before it takes effect, and we tell you the date it starts.

16. Contact

Every question, request, and complaint under this notice goes to contact@kairoks.com. Kairoks is an online service and email is our designated channel for receiving them.
